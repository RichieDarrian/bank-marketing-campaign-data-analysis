Performed commercial bank marketing campaign data analysis in R to identify data quality issues, customer response patterns, and campaign performance trends.

Key work:
- Performed data quality assessment covering missing values, duplicates, inconsistent categories, outliers, and noisy values.
- Cleaned and transformed the dataset through missing-value handling, median/mode imputation, categorical encoding, and feature engineering.
- Explored relationships between campaign outcomes and customer attributes using correlation analysis, cross-tabulation, and grouped summaries.
- Created features including age groups, contact recency levels, and total contact scores.
- Developed interactive Plotly visualizations to analyze subscription rates across workforce categories, call duration, age groups, and campaign months.
- Applied data storytelling techniques to communicate key findings and formulate data-driven business recommendations.
